# Family Continuity Test Scene v2

scene_id: test-family-living-room-v2
cast_id: family-cast-v1
style_id: family-anime-v2

## Goal

Test both identity continuity and the new rendering target:

1. four characters remain distinct and recognizable
2. characters have convincing three-dimensional volume
3. children look full, soft, healthy, and energetic
4. the image feels like warm hand-drawn feature animation rather than flat digital illustration

## Scene

A warm, tidy family living room.

- Mom sits on a sofa and gently holds Xiner.
- Xiner is sleepy and calm in Mom's arms.
- Grandma sits or crouches beside Anzai and helps him put toys away.
- Anzai holds a small toy fish and looks slightly reluctant but cooperative.

## Composition

- medium-wide storytelling shot
- all four characters clearly visible
- natural eye-level camera
- 35–50 mm equivalent perspective
- foreground toys for depth
- family in the middle ground
- soft painted domestic background
- no dramatic lens distortion

## Volume pass criteria

- faces show rounded cheek, jaw, nose, and chin planes
- soft form shadows reveal the shape of faces and bodies
- arms and legs feel cylindrical and weight-bearing, not flat
- clothing wraps around body volume
- contact points show gentle compression and occlusion
- seated and crouching poses show believable body weight
- children feel soft and full rather than thin
- poses are asymmetrical and alive

## Identity pass criteria

- Anzai remains a 2–3-year-old toddler with a round face, full cheeks, very short buzz-cut hair, slightly narrow dark eyes, and a small mouth with subtle pout tendency.
- Xiner remains clearly a very young infant with a very round face, very full cheeks, sparse fine hair, soft narrow eyes, and a small mouth often slightly open.
- Mom remains clearly younger than Grandma, with a natural oval-round face and casually tied-back dark hair.
- Grandma remains clearly older than Mom, with lively eyes, a warm smile, and gathered dark hair with some gray.
- No facial blending between Mom and Grandma.
- No age drift.
- No hairstyle drift.
- No generic chibi redesign.

## Failure notes to record after generation

- which character drifted
- what identity feature drifted
- whether faces or bodies looked flat
- whether children looked too thin
- whether poses looked stiff
- whether lighting failed to reveal form
- which agent/model was used
- whether reference images were attached
- what correction restored identity and volume
