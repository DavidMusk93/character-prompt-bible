# Family Continuity Test Scene v3

scene_id: test-family-living-room-v3
cast_id: family-cast-v1
style_id: family-anime-v3

## Goal

Test four things simultaneously:

1. the output reads clearly as 2D anime, not realistic or semi-realistic
2. the four locked characters remain distinct and recognizable
3. characters have stronger three-dimensional form through cel shading and overlap
4. expressions remain gentle and restrained, especially Grandma

## Scene

A warm, tidy family living room.

- Mom holds Xiner calmly.
- Xiner rests sleepily in Mom's arms.
- Grandma helps Anzai put toys away.
- Anzai holds a small toy fish and looks focused, mildly reluctant, but cooperative.

## Style pass criteria

- unmistakably anime / hand-drawn animation
- no photographic skin texture
- no semi-realistic portrait rendering
- clean organic linework
- painted animation background
- warm classic cel-animation feeling

## Volume pass criteria

- faces have rounded cheek and jaw turns
- shadow under nose, lower lip, chin, and bangs
- arms and legs read as rounded forms
- clothing wraps around body volume
- body overlaps create depth
- contact shadows appear where bodies touch
- seated and crouching poses show weight
- children feel soft, full, and healthy
- foreground / middle-ground / background separation is clear

## Expression pass criteria

- Mom: calm and affectionate
- Xiner: sleepy and peaceful
- Anzai: focused and mildly reluctant
- Grandma: gentle restrained smile

Grandma must not:
- laugh broadly
- show a huge open-mouth grin
- look overexcited
- become a caricature

## Identity pass criteria

- Anzai remains toddler-aged with round face, full cheeks, short buzz-cut hair, slightly narrow eyes, and subtle pout tendency.
- Xiner remains a very young infant with very round cheeks and sparse fine hair.
- Mom remains clearly younger than Grandma.
- Grandma remains clearly older than Mom and visually distinct.
- no facial blending
- no age drift
- no hairstyle drift

## Failure notes to record

- whether output drifted toward realism
- whether faces still looked flat
- whether body overlap and shadows were sufficient
- whether Grandma's smile became too large
- whether any identity drift occurred
- which model / agent was used
- whether reference images were attached
