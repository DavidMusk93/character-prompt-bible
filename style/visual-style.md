# Visual Style v1

style_id: family-anime-v1

## Direction

Warm contemporary Chinese-family anime illustration with a gentle picture-book feeling.

## Rendering

- clean, rounded line work
- controlled cel shading
- soft painterly secondary shading
- simplified but believable materials
- expressive faces without exaggerated manga deformation
- child-friendly proportions
- natural indoor and outdoor lighting
- moderate detail; avoid photorealism

## Shape language

- rounded silhouettes
- soft corners
- simple readable forms
- faces remain anatomically consistent between shots
- hands and feet simplified, but not chibi

## Camera

Preferred:
- eye-level or child-eye-level framing
- 35–50 mm equivalent perspective
- natural family-photo composition
- medium shots and medium-wide shots

Avoid:
- extreme fisheye
- heroic low-angle distortion
- fashion-editorial poses
- exaggerated anime foreshortening

## Lighting

- soft daylight
- warm household practical lights
- restrained highlights
- no neon cyberpunk lighting unless a scene explicitly requires it

## Texture

- subtle paper-like softness is acceptable
- no heavy watercolor bleeding
- no glossy 3D-render skin
- no plastic toy-like surfaces

## Consistency rule

This file controls the **rendering language** only. It must not redefine character identity.
