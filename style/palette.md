# Palette v1

style_palette_id: family-anime-palette-v1

## Base palette

Use muted, warm, low-saturation colors.

- warm cream: #F3E7D3
- soft yellow: #D9B45B
- muted navy: #42536B
- dusty blue: #738CA6
- warm brown: #7B5D4A
- soft sage: #8E9A7B
- gentle coral: #C98372
- charcoal: #343434

## Skin

Use natural East Asian skin tones with warm-neutral undertones.
Do not push skin strongly pink, orange, gray, or porcelain-white.

## Rule

Signature clothing colors defined in character files take precedence over scene palette choices.
