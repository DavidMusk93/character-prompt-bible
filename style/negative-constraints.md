# Global Negative Constraints

These constraints are model-agnostic.

## Identity drift

Avoid:
- changing age appearance
- changing face shape
- changing eye geometry
- changing hairstyle silhouette
- changing body proportions
- changing signature clothing colors without explicit wardrobe override
- turning a character into a different ethnicity
- adult facial structure on children
- child facial structure on adults

## Style drift

Avoid:
- photorealism
- semi-realistic portrait rendering
- realistic skin texture
- visible pores
- photographic wrinkles
- glossy 3D animation rendering
- game-render skin
- hyper-realistic hair strands
- extreme chibi
- hyper-detailed seinen line art
- cyberpunk color grading
- fashion illustration
- sterile vector art
- flat paper-doll anatomy
- flat front lighting
- shadowless faces
- plastic-looking highlights

## Expression drift

Avoid:
- exaggerated open-mouth laughter
- giant toothy grin
- comedy reaction face
- overexcited expressions
- all characters smiling broadly at the same time

For Grandma specifically:
- no exaggerated broad laughter
- no huge open-mouth grin
- no caricatured cheerful-old-lady face

## Character vitality failures

Avoid:
- thin, deflated-looking cheeks on children
- weak or under-constructed limbs
- stiff symmetrical poses
- mannequin posture
- every character staring at the camera
- lifeless facial expressions
- weightless seated poses
- hands that do not convincingly contact objects
- overly delicate fashion-model anatomy

## Volume failures

Avoid:
- flat face with no cheek turn
- flat torso with no overlap
- flat arms with no cylindrical mass
- clothing pasted onto the body
- no contact shadows
- no shadow under chin or nose
- no foreground / middle-ground / background separation

## Common generation defects

Avoid:
- extra fingers
- fused hands
- duplicated limbs
- inconsistent shoe design
- mismatched eyes
- asymmetric facial identity
- accidental text
- watermarks
