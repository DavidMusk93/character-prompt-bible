# Xiner

> Status: **locked v1**. This character definition is frozen for continuity use. Permanent face, hair, age, and body-proportion changes require a new version such as `xiner-v2`.

character_id: xiner-v1

## Role

Baby girl in the family cast.

## Identity

- very young Chinese infant girl
- very round baby face
- very full cheeks
- soft, narrow dark eyes, often sleepy or half-closed
- very light, fine eyebrows
- small rounded nose
- small mouth, often slightly open
- visibly small-infant proportions

## Hair

- near-black
- sparse and very fine
- close to the scalp
- soft natural hairline
- no styled bangs
- no thick or voluminous hair

## Body

- infant proportions
- head large relative to body
- short, soft limbs
- short neck and rounded shoulder line
- naturally curled or supported resting poses

## Locked anchors

- small-infant age appearance
- very round face
- very full cheeks
- sparse fine near-black hair
- soft narrow eyes
- small rounded nose
- small mouth often slightly open
- infant body proportions

## Common expressions and states

- sleepy
- calm
- half-awake
- sleeping with mouth slightly open
- quietly observant
- soft, secure, milk-baby feeling

## Wardrobe references

- red sleeveless / strap-style baby top
- white breathable baby outfit with pale pink detail
- white baby outfit with soft pale-yellow dot accents
- cream or pale-yellow hooded baby outfit

Wardrobe may change by scene. Face, hair, age, and proportions may not.

## Strong don'ts

Avoid:
- toddler-like posture
- older-baby facial proportions
- thick hair or bangs
- oversized sparkling anime eyes
- pointed chin
- mature expressions
- standing or independent walking poses
