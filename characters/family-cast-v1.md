# Family Cast v1

cast_id: family-cast-v1
style_id: family-anime-v3
status: locked

## Members

- anzai-v1
- xiner-v1
- mom-v1
- grandma-v1

## Continuity contract

These four character definitions are the canonical source of identity.

Priority order:

```text
approved reference image
> locked character spec
> shared visual style
> scene prompt
> image-agent-specific syntax
```

## Character independence

Do not blend traits between characters.

- **Anzai**: toddler boy; round face; full cheeks; very short buzz-cut hair; slightly narrow dark eyes; small rounded nose; subtle pout tendency.
- **Xiner**: very young infant girl; very round face; very full cheeks; sparse fine hair; soft narrow eyes; small mouth often slightly open.
- **Mom**: young mother; natural oval-round face; gentle narrow eyes; casually tied-back dark hair with loose strands; understated daily-life appearance.
- **Grandma**: grandmother; round-to-oval face; lively eyes; warm gentle smile; gathered dark hair with some gray and loose strands; energetic practical presence.

## Shared rendering rules

Use `family-anime-v3`.

The family should look:
- unmistakably 2D anime
- hand-drawn
- warm
- rounded
- full and lively
- physically present through cel-shaded volume
- expressive but gentle
- never semi-realistic

## Volume rules

Depth must come from animation drawing and cel shading, not realism.

Require:
- rounded cheek and limb construction
- 3-value cel shading
- shadow under nose, lower lip, chin, bangs, sleeves, and overlapping limbs
- contact shadows where bodies touch
- clothing wrapping around body volume
- visible body weight in seated and crouching poses
- clear near / middle / far planes

## Expression rules

Default expressions should be:
- warm
- gentle
- restrained
- attentive
- natural

Grandma:
- gentle smile by default
- no broad open-mouth laughter
- no exaggerated toothy grin

## Locked rules

Unless a new version is intentionally created, do not change:
- age appearance
- face shape
- eye geometry
- hair silhouette
- body proportions
- each character's distinguishing identity anchors

Wardrobe, pose, expression, camera, lighting, and environment may change by scene.

## Anti-drift rules

Avoid:
- photorealism
- semi-realistic portrait rendering
- realistic skin texture
- oversized sparkling anime eyes
- pointed chins
- idol-style beautification
- fashion-model proportions
- dramatic age drift
- random hairstyle redesign
- generic chibi conversion
- flat cutout-like characters
- broad exaggerated laughter
- blending Mom and Grandma into the same face
- making Xiner look toddler-aged
- making Anzai look school-aged
