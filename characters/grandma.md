# Grandma

> Status: **locked v1**. This character definition is frozen for continuity use. Permanent face, hair, age, and body-proportion changes require a new version such as `grandma-v2`.

character_id: grandma-v1

## Role

Grandmother in the family cast.

## Identity

- Chinese grandmother
- round-to-oval face
- full, natural facial volume
- lively dark eyes
- warm, highly recognizable smile, often showing teeth
- natural age lines around eyes and mouth
- rounded natural nose
- energetic, capable family-elder presence

## Hair

- dark hair with some visible gray
- usually tied or gathered back simply
- natural loose strands around forehead and sides
- practical home-life silhouette
- no elaborate styling

## Body

- natural older-adult proportions
- upright, active posture
- comfortable and experienced child-holding poses
- practical hand gestures

## Locked anchors

- grandmother age appearance
- round-to-oval face
- lively eyes
- warm tooth-showing smile
- tied or gathered dark hair with some gray and loose strands
- energetic, practical, capable family-elder presence

## Common expressions and states

- open warm smile
- cheerful household activity
- focused attention on a child
- relaxed while holding or guiding a child

## Wardrobe references

- loose light-colored T-shirt
- dark gray / striped household apron or work layer
- blue-green striped knit top for cooler-weather scenes
- practical dark trousers

Wardrobe may change by scene, but should remain practical and life-like.

## Strong don'ts

Avoid:
- making her frail or extremely elderly
- very thin pointed face
- glamorous styling
- heavy makeup
- elaborate curls
- caricatured old-lady features
- passive or sickly presentation unless a scene explicitly requires it
