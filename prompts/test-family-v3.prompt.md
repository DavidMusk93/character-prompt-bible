Use the locked family cast definitions:

- xiner-v1

- family-anime-v3

Preserve exact continuity with the approved reference images and locked character specifications.

Do not redesign faces, age appearance, hairstyle silhouettes, body proportions, or family identity.

## Mandatory visual style

Create a **clearly 2D anime illustration**.

The output must look like classic hand-drawn Japanese feature animation, with warm Studio Ghibli-like domestic atmosphere and traditional cel-animation language.

Do not make it realistic or semi-realistic.

Do not use photographic skin texture.

Do not copy any specific existing film, character, costume, frame, or composition.

## Anime rendering

Use:
- clean organic hand-drawn linework
- variable line weight
- simplified anime facial features
- rounded appealing character shapes
- 3-value cel shading: light / midtone / shadow
- hand-painted watercolor / gouache-like background
- warm soft palette
- gentle natural expressions
- clear silhouette readability

## Stronger three-dimensional form

The characters must still feel physically three-dimensional even though the style is fully anime.

Achieve volume through:
- rounded skull and cheek masses
- visible cheek turn and jaw turn
- shadow under nose, lower lip, chin, and bangs
- rounded cylindrical arms and legs
- overlap between forearm / torso / lap / hands
- contact shadows where bodies touch
- clothing wrapping around body masses
- slight perspective scaling between near and far limbs
- clear foreground, middle-ground, and background separation

Do not use realistic skin texture to create volume.

## Scene

A warm, tidy living room in a real family home.

Mom is sitting on a sofa or low on the floor, gently holding baby Xiner in her arms.

Xiner is sleepy and calm.

Grandma is beside Anzai, sitting or crouching near him and helping him put toys away.

Anzai holds a small toy fish and looks slightly reluctant but cooperative.
