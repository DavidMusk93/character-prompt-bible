## Visual direction

Create a warm, cinematic, hand-drawn Japanese feature-animation illustration with a classic cel-painted feeling.

The target is not flat cartoon art.

Characters must feel rounded, full, lively, and physically present in the room.

Use:
- organic hand-drawn contour lines
- variable line weight
- 2–3 clear cel-shading value groups
- soft secondary bounce light
- warm skin with subtle color variation
- visible facial and body planes
- gentle contact shadows
- painted watercolor / gouache-like background treatment
- convincing foreground, middle-ground, and background depth

Do not copy any specific existing film, frame, costume, or character design.

## Character volume requirements

Faces:
- rounded skull and cheek volume
- visible side planes of cheeks and jaw
- soft shadow under nose, lower lip, and chin
- subtle warm light on forehead and cheekbone
- no flat front-lit faces

Bodies:
- shoulders, upper arms, forearms, hands, torso, thighs, and knees should read as rounded forms
- clothing must wrap around the body instead of appearing pasted on
- seated and crouching bodies must show believable weight and compression

Children:
- cheeks and limbs should feel soft, full, healthy, and energetic
- no thin or deflated child anatomy
- cute through natural proportions, not giant anime eyes

## Scene

A warm, tidy living room in a real family home.

Mom is sitting on a sofa, gently holding baby Xiner in her arms.

Xiner looks sleepy and calm. She has a very round infant face, very full cheeks, sparse fine near-black hair close to the scalp, soft narrow dark eyes, a small rounded nose, and a small mouth slightly open. She must clearly look like a very young infant.

Grandma is beside Anzai, sitting or crouching near him and helping him put toys away.

Anzai is a 2–3-year-old toddler boy with a round face, full cheeks, very short near-black buzz-cut hair, slightly narrow dark eyes, a small rounded nose, and a small mouth with a subtle pout tendency. He is holding a small toy fish and looks slightly reluctant but cooperative.

Mom is a young Chinese mother with a natural oval-round face, gentle narrow dark eyes, casually tied-back near-black hair with a few loose strands, simple understated home clothes, and a calm caring expression.

Grandma is a Chinese grandmother with a round-to-oval face, lively dark eyes, a warm recognizable smile, dark hair with some gray gathered back simply with natural loose strands, and a practical energetic family-elder presence.

## Pose and vitality

Make the moment feel candid and alive:

- Anzai slightly twists his torso while holding the toy fish.
- Grandma leans toward him with one hand actively guiding toys into the basket.
- Mom naturally shifts her weight to support Xiner.
- Xiner rests heavily and securely in Mom's arms.
- Head angles, shoulders, elbows, knees, and hands should be slightly asymmetrical.
- Nobody should look like a posed mannequin.
- Not everyone should look directly at the camera.

## Lighting

Use warm directional window light from one side, with soft cool ambient fill from the room.

Add:
- gentle shadow under chins
- cheek and nose form shadows
- soft occlusion where arms touch bodies
- reflected warm light from floor and furniture
- slightly darker room corners for depth

Keep the result warm and inviting, not dramatic.

## Background

Use a softly painted domestic background:
- sofa
- warm wood
- toy basket
- a few scattered toys
- fabric cushions
- plants or simple household details

Keep environmental edges softer than the character faces.

Use foreground overlap and background value separation to create depth.

## Identity rules

Keep each character independently recognizable.

Do not blend Mom and Grandma.

Keep Mom clearly younger than Grandma.

Keep Xiner clearly much younger than Anzai.

Keep Anzai clearly toddler-aged.

Do not change permanent face geometry, hair silhouette, age, or body proportions.
